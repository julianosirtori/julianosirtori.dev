I have used Obsidian for a while and I wanted to read, add, and edit my notes from ChatGPT, from anywhere and on any device where I open the chat. While researching, I found options like [MCP-Obsidian](https://github.com/MarkusPfundstein/mcp-obsidian), but almost all of them had the same catch. Either I had to leave my computer on, or they required installing the ChatGPT app on my machine.

## Non-negotiable requirements

Before writing any code, I wrote down what I would not compromise on:

* It must work in the ChatGPT mobile app.
* It must work with other models, like Claude chat.
* It must keep working with my computer turned off.
* I will install nothing on my computer.
* It must open on any device signed in to my ChatGPT account.

From that list, two decisions were clear. I had to host the MCP. And it needed [OAuth](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/build/auth#publish-oauth-metadata-from-your-authorization-server) authentication.

## A gatekeeper in front of the MCP

The sensitive part was security. A leak would expose all my notes to the internet. To handle that, I placed a gatekeeper in front of the MCP. That gatekeeper knows nothing about the vault. It handles OAuth authentication and forwards only authenticated requests to the MCP.

As an extra layer, the **mcp-server** requires the worker to send a header (`x-origin-secret`) on every request. Only the worker and the mcp-server know that secret.

## Two months in

I have used it for two months and it works better than I expected. It still needs polish, like a nicer OAuth screen, but it is functional and that is what matters. On my roadmap I also want to simplify the VPS setup. For non-technical folks, I think it is still too complicated. I believe prebuilt Docker images can fix that.

If you want to install it already, follow this [tutorial](https://github.com/julianosirtori/vault-mcp/blob/main/docs/setup.pt-br.md).

Suggestions are always welcome.
